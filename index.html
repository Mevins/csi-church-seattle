Welcome

Growing together in Christ

CSI Church of Seattle is a congregation of the Church of South India (CSI).

We gather together for worship, prayer, Holy Communion, and Christian fellowship. Our services provide an opportunity for families and individuals to come together, encourage one another, and celebrate our faith.

Everyone is warmly invited to join us.
